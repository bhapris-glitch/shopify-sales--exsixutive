Refund & Cancellation Policy

Last updated: September 29, 2026

This policy explains cancellation and refund requests for zavoka AI subscriptions. It should be read with our Terms & Conditions. Applicable consumer law takes precedence where it gives you rights that cannot be waived.

Free trial

The public offer is a 5-day Premium trial with up to 50 AI chats, whichever limit is reached first. No payment is taken during the trial, and it does not automatically convert into a paid subscription. When the trial ends or its chat limit is reached, the service pauses unless you separately activate a paid plan. There is no trial payment to refund.

Canceling a paid subscription

You can turn off autopay in the Billing section of the merchant dashboard. Cancellation stops the next renewal; your paid access normally continues until the end of the already-paid billing period. To avoid a renewal, turn off autopay before the renewal is processed. You may also request cancellation at contact@zavoka.com. Cancellation does not itself refund a payment already made.

Refund requests

All plans: Payments for Starter, Growth, Premium, and any other paid plan are non-refundable except where applicable law gives you a right to cancel, withdraw, or receive a refund. If the law applicable to your purchase gives you a 14-day cooling-off or withdrawal right, you may request a refund within that legally applicable 14-day period, subject to any lawful conditions or exceptions. The 14-day period applies only where and to the extent that such a right applies; it is not a voluntary worldwide refund window. Where applicable law gives you a different period or other mandatory remedy, we will honor that requirement.

To exercise a legally applicable refund or withdrawal right, email contact@zavoka.com within the deadline required by the law that applies to you and include your Shopify store URL, billing email, payment date, plan, and a clear request. A refund request does not cancel a subscription or stop future renewals; cancel separately. If a refund is legally required, it is normally sent to the original payment method; bank and payment-provider processing times vary. We may ask for information needed to verify the transaction and request.

Billing errors and legal rights

If you believe you were charged incorrectly, contact us promptly with the transaction details so we can investigate. Nothing in this policy limits mandatory statutory rights, including any applicable cooling-off, withdrawal, or refund rights under consumer law. Where a legal right conflicts with this policy, the legal right applies.

Contact

Questions about a cancellation or refund? Contact contact@zavoka.com.

Confirm the final billing flow, payment-provider rules, and consumer-law requirements for each market before publication. This policy is a general service disclosure, not legal advice.
